# Customer discovery outreach

Use these as individual messages, not bulk spam. Personalize the opening with a real, relevant detail from the agency's public website. Ask for permission to interview; do not imply that the agency is already a customer.

## WhatsApp or LinkedIn first message

Hi, I'm building an early workflow tool for Kenyan letting and property-management teams. It is designed to help staff track inquiries, remember client requirements, and avoid claiming a listing is available unless the agency has recorded a verification check.

I'm not asking you to buy anything. Could I ask you 5 short questions about how your team handles rental inquiries and viewing follow-ups? If it looks relevant, I can show you a short demo using sample data. If you're busy, no problem.

## Email subject
Research request: rental inquiry and follow-up workflow

## Email body

Hello,

I'm developing an early-stage CRM workflow for Kenyan letting and property-management teams. I'm researching how agencies currently manage inquiries, client preferences, listing availability checks, and viewing requests.

Would someone on your team be open to a 15-minute discovery conversation? I'm looking for honest feedback, including reasons the tool would not be useful. I can demonstrate the current prototype with sample data, but I won't ask you to share tenant records or confidential information.

Thank you for considering it.

Regards,
Estate AI Agent CRM

## After the interview

Thank you for explaining your process. I wrote down the main problem as: [repeat their problem in their words]. Did I understand correctly?

If you're willing, I'd like to return with a small demo focused only on that workflow and agree on how we'd measure whether it helps. No customer data is needed for the first demo.

## Pilot invitation

Based on our conversation, I can offer a 14-day, tightly scoped design-partner pilot. We'd agree on one workflow, use sample or explicitly consented data, set a baseline, review failures twice a week, and compare results at the end. There is no promise of a result; the goal is to measure it honestly. If it does not help, we'll document why and stop.

## Objection: "We already have software"

That makes sense. I'm not asking you to replace it. Which part of inquiry handling or follow-up still takes manual effort, if any? If the current setup already solves it, that's useful feedback and I won't try to force a fit.

## Objection: "Send me details"

Happy to. The current prototype focuses on inquiry tracking, saved preferences, property records with verification notes, and human approval holds. It does not yet have real calendar booking or full multi-agency account isolation, so I would only show it with sample data for now. Would a short demo be useful?

## Follow-up etiquette

- Send at most one polite follow-up after 5–7 business days.
- If there is no response or they say no, stop.
- Don't scrape personal contact details or add people to marketing lists without permission.
- Don't promise revenue increases, legal verification, autonomous bookings, or production security that has not been demonstrated.
